I am a born creative strategist who grew up in Dubai, studied architecture and design in the UK, and has since built a career that refuses to stay in a single lane.

In under two years working in Dubai, I moved from a leading social media agency, where I learned to direct content, build brand worlds, and work across wellness, F&B, fashion and beauty clients, to a fast-moving startup, to heading up marketing for a luxury real estate firm.

Before that: the fitting rooms of Jimmy Choo, Dior and Calvin Klein in England, where I truly learned luxury from the inside out. That perspective evolved further at Soho House, where exposure to design-led interiors and members-only culture refined my understanding of what true exclusivity and taste feel like.

I am currently based in India, while staying connected to the global brands and briefs that have shaped my work. I believe the most exciting creative frontiers live where culture, technology and storytelling collide.

I believe in quality over quantity.

HKW Wealth: Brand Strategy & Transformation

A complete brand transformation shaped around one central ambition: positioning HKW Wealth as a distinguished name in the ultra-high-net-worth space.

Working closely with the CEO and COO, I translated their vision into a cohesive luxury brand, rethinking everything from the logo and visual identity to the website and wider digital presence. Every creative decision was guided by a focus on quality, discretion and understated luxury, ensuring the brand reflected the calibre of clientele it aimed to attract.

My role spanned creative direction, brand positioning and the development of a consistent visual language across touchpoints, bringing the leadership team’s vision to life through a more considered and elevated brand experience.

Made by handThe identity was developed in close collaboration with a professional graphic designer: every mark, layout and asset was drawn, refined and finished by people, without the use of AI.

01

Logo & identity

A monogram built from the initials, set in gold and kept deliberately quiet: one mark, a handful of approved variations and clear rules on how it may be used. Developed with a professional graphic designer and refined by hand over several rounds, with no AI-generated elements.

02

Colour & type

A palette anchored by gold, softened by warm neutrals and grounded in deep charcoal, paired with Fellix for headlines and Degular for reading.

03

Business cards

The identity in the hand: a brushed-gold face carrying only the monogram, and a white reverse where an oversized monogram crops off the edge beside the contact details.

04

Imagery & voice

Architecture, marble, golden light and natural texture; people used sparingly. Words that sound like counsel rather than sales.
